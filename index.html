Sevenfold Botanical Scalp Balm

A handcrafted balm made with seven purposeful ingredients:

  • 1. Virgin Coconut Oil
  • 2. Shea Butter
  • 3. Castor Oil
  • 4. Beeswax
  • 5. Vitamin E
  • 6. Tea Tree Oil
  • 7. Rosemary Oil

Apply a very small amount (about the size of a grain of rice) and massage it directly into the scalp. Let it sit for 20 minutes, then shampoo out. Do not apply heavily to the hair strands.

Our botanical balm is made with pure, unrefined ingredients. It is normal for the balm to soften or melt in transit during hot summer months. If this happens, simply place the jar in the refrigerator for 15 minutes to restore its solid texture.

Formulated for the resilient skin of the scalp. If you have highly acne-prone facial skin, avoid dragging the product down onto your face.
